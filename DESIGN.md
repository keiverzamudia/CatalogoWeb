## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, industrial-corporate visual identity engineered for logistics, energy, lubricants, and commercial transport operations. It bridges institutional dependability with high-speed field utility—optimized for direct access via corporate event QR codes, product catalog lookup, and rapid B2B quotation.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** structure with **High-Contrast High-Performance** accents. Solid foundational geometry gives credibility and authority, while electric lime highlights provide immediate visual momentum, high legibility under direct sunlight on handheld devices, and distinct interactive feedback.

Visual tone attributes:
- **Resilient & Authoritative:** Sturdy grids, confident typography, and dark navy anchors evoke structural strength.
- **Fast & Utilitarian:** Minimal decorative chrome, tight micro-interactions, high data density without clutter.
- **Dynamic Field-Ready:** High contrast optimized for outdoor inspection, trade show mobile scanning, and swift catalog filtering.

## Colors

The palette balances a commanding corporate foundation with a high-energy kinetic accent:

- **Primary Navy (`#003366`):** The institutional anchor. Used for primary branding surfaces, high-priority buttons, hero banners, dark-mode cards, active segmented controls, and deep structural footers.
- **Secondary Lime (`#95c800`):** The dynamic trigger. Used for high-conversion action tags, highlight text in display headlines, positive status indicators, QR landing badges, and focused active tabs.
- **Neutral Light Base (`#f8fafc` & `#ffffff`):** Pure white serves as the primary card and container surface, while Slate 50 (`#f8fafc`) sets a restful, glare-resistant screen background.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#e2e8f0`):** Subtle slate line treatment establishing architectural boundaries without visual noise.
- **Typography Neutrals (`#0f172a` & `#64748b`):** Deep Slate (`#0f172a`) ensures optimal legibility for titles and primary data points, while Slate (`#64748b`) handles secondary technical metadata, serial codes, and field labels.
- **State Semantics:**
  - *Success / Active:* `#95c800` (Lime Accent)
  - *Warning / Hazard:* `#f59e0b` (Amber)
  - *Critical / Error:* `#ef4444` (Ruby)
  - *Info / Status:* `#003366` (Navy Tone)

## Typography

The single-family typographic architecture relies on **Rubik**, exploiting its dual personality:

1. **Commercial & Industrial Impact (ExtraBold Italic):**
   Used for primary section heroes, key performance stats, and promotional product category heads. The dynamic forward slant conveys speed, logistics capability, and transport power.
2. **Operational Clarity (Regular, Medium, SemiBold):**
   Used across product specs, lot sizes, stock indicators, and mobile form controls. Rubik’s subtle rounded corners maintain warmth and high screen legibility even at compact 11px and 12px sizes on mobile displays.

Headlines employ tight leading to preserve vertical density. Secondary tracking is slightly opened (+0.04em) on micro-labels and badges to ensure instantaneous legibility during quick trade show interactions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, prioritizing one-handed thumb navigation and ultra-fast discovery when accessed through printed QR codes:

- **Mobile Viewport (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layouts, 4-column sub-grid for catalog items, edge-to-edge category pill scrollers with snap-scrolling, and fixed bottom action bars for high-intent quotation workflows.
- **Tablet & Mid-tier (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Split quotation cards and 2-to-3 column product tiles.
- **Desktop Viewport (> 1024px):** 12-column structured grid centered within a maximum width of `1280px`. Left-rail persistent filters and right-side catalog displays.

A consistent 4px/8px modular rhythm governs component internals: cards employ `space-md` internal padding, while actionable elements maintain minimum 44px tap targets for frictionless field use.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through crisp surface separation rather than heavy skeuomorphic shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** Tone `#f8fafc` — un-elevated base layer.
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#ffffff` with a hairline stroke `1px solid #e2e8f0` and an ambient, low-contrast shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Pressed Cards):** Elevated micro-card state: `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(0, 51, 102, 0.08)`. Border shifts dynamically to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Drawers, Bottom Sheets & Sticky CTAs):** `box-shadow: 0 -4px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.10)` on a pure white or Navy `#003366` surface.
- **Brand Elevation (Hero & Dark Modules):** High-contrast background container in `#003366`, utilizing crisp white and `#95c800` typography overlays to create visual depth and focal weight without layer diffusion.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric balance: structural items hold steady medium radii, while interactive and navigational elements utilize pill geometry:

- **Structural Containers & Product Cards:** Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px) radius. This maintains an industrial, organized appearance suitable for machinery, lubricants, and commercial vehicle parts.
- **Buttons, Filter Chips & Status Badges:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) to clearly signal touch interactivity and create tactile contrast against rectangular content cards.
- **Input Fields & Dropdowns:** `0.5rem` (8px) to match container harmony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Corporate Navy `#003366` background, crisp white typography (`label-lg`), pill-shaped. Hover/active states trigger a micro-shift with navy deepening to `#002244`.
- **Accent Action:** Vibrancy Lime `#95c800` background, dark Slate `#0f172a` typography (`label-lg`), pill-shaped. Reserved for high-value conversions: "Cotizar ahora", "Ver catálogo", or QR scan confirmations.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** White background, hairline border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, navy text. Hover introduces `#f8fafc` tint.
- **Floating WhatsApp / Assistant:** Circular or rounded pill floating triggers anchored at the bottom-right, sporting brand green `#25D366` or lime `#95c800` with subtle elevation.

### Filter Pills & Segmented Controls
- **Category Pills:** Pill-shaped, horizontally scrollable containers for event mobile browsing.
  - *Inactive:* Pure white background, `1px solid #e2e8f0`, text `#64748b`.
  - *Active:* Solid Corporate Navy `#003366` background, white text with secondary lime item count indicator.
- **Segmented Unit Tabs:** Top-level nav pills styled as high-contrast switches (e.g., "Combustibles", "Lubricantes y repuestos"). Active segments feature `#95c800` background with `#0f172a` text.

### Product & Equipment Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white surface, 1px `#e2e8f0` outer border, `0.5rem` radius.
- **Image Frame:** Top anchor with square 1:1 or 4:3 aspect ratio, subtle gray photo-bed, overlaid with a floating category badge (e.g., "MOTOS", "CAMIÓN", "AUTOS") positioned at top-left.
- **Metadata Stack:** Compact layout consisting of:
  - Technical sub-headline in `#64748b` uppercase (`label-sm`).
  - Bold product name in `#0f172a` (`headline-sm`).
  - Dark Navy badge pill displaying internal SKU/Part number (`#003366` background, white text).
  - Packaging/Liters spec in `#64748b`.
- **Card CTA:** Full-width bottom button: Navy outline or solid button reading "Cotizar este producto".

### Badges & Category Tags
- Pill geometry with uppercase micro-copy (`label-sm`).
- Pre-defined role colors:
  - *Industrial Heavy / Truck:* Navy tone `#003366` with white text.
  - *Automotive / Speed:* Accent lime `#95c800` with dark Slate text.
  - *Industrial Safety / Maintenance:* Amber tint background with deep amber label.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Global Search:** Prominent rounded-full search bar with a magnifying icon at `#64748b`, crisp white fill, and subtle slate border. Focus state activates a 2px outline in `#003366`.
- **Quotation Form Fields:** `0.5rem` rounded corners, 44px height, slate borders, light `#f8fafc` interior fill when idle, transitioning to white on focus.

### Metric / Stat Highlights
- Compact hero blocks emphasizing fast fulfillment: "24-48 h Entrega", "+1.200 Referencias", "Marcas Oficiales". Numbers set in Rubik ExtraBold Italic `#95c800` or `#003366`.
